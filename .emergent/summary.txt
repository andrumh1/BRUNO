<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to continue the development of a digital business card application, based on a previous design. The goal is to build a comprehensive platform with the following features:
1.  **Card Types:** Four initial types (Personal, Corporate, Business, Places), with a fifth (Guardian) added later.
2.  **Card Design & Sharing:**
    *   Blurred bubble background.
    *   Focus on card templates, with at least 3 templates per card type (15 total).
    *   Permanent, shareable links and QR codes for each card. QR codes should feature the BRUNO logo.
    *   Ability to upload images for profile avatar, banner, and photo albums from the device or a profile-specific album.
    *   Social media links should be displayed on the card with their respective logos.
3.  **User Profiles & Management:**
    *   A central user profile to store contact info, social media links, etc., which can be selectively added to cards.
    *   Different profile levels: Public (free), Corporate (admin manages employee cards), Business, and a Super-admin for the owner.
4.  **Social & Engagement Features:**
    *   A public Feed or Explore page to view public cards, with features like comments, ratings, and likes. This should be the main page of the app.
    *   A system to save/collect cards (history, favorites, saved).
    *   A map view to see the locations of saved cards.
5.  **NFC Integration:** A feature to activate and write to physical NFC cards from within the app.
6.  **App & Branding:**
    *   Integration of the user-provided BRUNO logos.
    *   A smart download button for PWA installation on different devices (Web, iOS, Android).
    *   Pages for Contact, Help/Support, and Subscriptions/Pricing.
    *   The app should be a PWA, with future consideration for native AppStore/PlayStore releases.
